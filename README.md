TouchGrass is an AI-powered outdoor exploration game designed to encourage people to spend less time on their screens and more time experiencing the world around them.

Using open-weight AI, TouchGrass generates short, fun outdoor missions that encourage users to step outside, observe their surroundings, appreciate nature, and earn points for completing activities.

Built for the Hacktoberfest 2026 Open-Source AI Challenge — Week 1: Touch Grass.

Details include:

AI-Generated Missions: Receive short outdoor challenges with three simple instructions.

Nature Exploration: Discover interesting details in your surroundings through observation-based activities.

Points System: Earn points when you complete missions.

Progress Tracking: Your current mission, completion status, and total points are saved using browser localStorage.

Local AI: Generate missions using Qwen 2.5 (1.5B) through Ollama without relying on a cloud AI API.

Online Demo: The deployed version uses OpenRouter for hosted AI inference, with predefined missions as a fallback when AI generation fails.

Responsive Design: Works across desktop and mobile screen sizes.

Privacy-Friendly: No account or personal information is required to play.
